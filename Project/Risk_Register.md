# Project Risk Register

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Possible Cause | Probability | Impact | Mitigation / Response Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Changing or unclear requirements | New requirements or changes may be introduced during development | Medium | Medium | The team will clearly document the agreed requirements and prioritize the core features. New changes can be considered in later increments. |
| R2 | Integration problems | Different increments or components may not work correctly together | Medium | High | The team will define how components interact and test each increment before integrating it with the rest of the system. |
| R3 | Schedule delays | Some development tasks may take longer than expected | Medium | High | The project will be divided into smaller tasks with deadlines, and progress will be monitored regularly. |
| R4 | Team member availability | Team members may have other coursework, exams, or personal commitments | Medium | Medium | Responsibilities will be shared between team members and project work will be documented so another member can continue if necessary. |
| R5 | Security or privacy concerns | TutorBook will contain user accounts and booking information | Low | High | The team will use proper authentication and access control and avoid storing unnecessary sensitive user information. |
